🚀 Core Features

🎯 Realistic Physics

Based on Roblox 2008 physics engine with gravity, friction, elasticity, and advanced collision detection.

3️⃣ 3D Visualization

Real-time 3D rendering with Phong lighting, interactive camera controls, and smooth animations.

🎨 Materials Library

Built-in materials: Plastic, Wood, Metal, Glass, Gold, Steel, and more with realistic properties.

🧩 Shape Variety

Create with cubes, spheres, cylinders, wedges, and more. Full customization of size, color, and properties.

💻 WebAssembly

High-performance physics simulation compiled to WebAssembly, running at near-native speeds in your browser.

🌍 Cross-Platform

Works on any modern browser supporting WebGL and WASM. Desktop, tablet, and mobile compatible.

📖 Quick Reference

Physics Engine

physics.cpp - Core 3D physics with collision detection and impulse response

3D Rendering

renderer.cpp - OpenGL-based visualization with lighting and shaders

Shape Builder

shape_builder.cpp - Interactive tool for creating and customizing objects

Web Integration

physics_wasm.cpp - WebAssembly bindings for browser physics

Material System

8+ built-in materials with unique physical properties for realistic simulation

Scene Format

JSON-based scene files for easy saving, loading, and sharing of creations

🎮 Getting Started

Follow these steps to get the most out of Boblox:

1️⃣ Explore Physics

Start with the physics playground to understand how gravity, collisions, and materials affect objects.

2️⃣ Visit Studio

Open the Studio editor to create your first scene. Drag objects, adjust properties, and test physics.

3️⃣ Make Games

Use the shape builder to create custom objects and scenes. Save and embed them permanently.

4️⃣ Share & Play

Upload your games to the community games page and play creations from other builders.
